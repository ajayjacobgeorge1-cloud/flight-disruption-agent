# IRROPS (Irregular Operations) Policy v3.2

## rebooking
When a flight is cancelled or delayed more than 90 minutes due to a
carrier-controlled reason (mechanical, crew, operational), the airline will
rebook the passenger on the next available flight in the same cabin class
at no additional cost. Passengers may request a higher cabin class, in
which case the fare difference applies and requires agent approval above
$750.

## hotel
Passengers on an overnight misconnect caused by a carrier-controlled
disruption are entitled to a hotel voucher (up to $150/night) and two
meal vouchers ($25 each) per affected night. Weather-caused disruptions
are NOT eligible for hotel compensation per DOT guidance, but the airline
offers a discounted hotel rate as a courtesy.

## compensation
Cash compensation (not vouchers) is only owed under EU261/UK261 rules for
flights departing the EU/UK, or under the carrier's own Customer Service
Plan for controllable delays over 3 hours on US domestic flights. Weather
and ATC-driven delays are excluded from cash compensation in all cases.

## escalation
Any rebooking action with a fare difference at or above $750, any request
involving a cabin-class upgrade beyond business class, or any compensation
claim disputing a carrier's weather/ATC classification must be escalated
to a human agent rather than resolved autonomously.
